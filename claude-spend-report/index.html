Claude spend report

Reads the spend report CSV exported from a Claude Team or Enterprise organization and shows how usage-credit spend is distributed across members, by product and by model, and who is at or over a per-member limit you type in. The file is parsed in this page. Nothing is uploaded and this page makes no network requests of any kind.

Both values are yours, typed here. Neither is in the export, so the figures that use them are labeled as based on what you typed.

Where the CSV comes from. An Owner or Primary Owner exports it from claude.ai: Settings → Analytics → “How much is Claude costing?” → Export spend report, choosing month to date, last month, the last 90 days or a custom range. The export is available once usage credits are turned on for the organization. No token is required.

Expected columns: user_email, product, model, total_requests, total_prompt_tokens, total_completion_tokens, total_net_spend_usd, total_gross_spend_usd, and, where present, an account UUID and a model family column. Columns are matched by header name; unexpected columns are ignored and missing ones are reported.

What the file carries. On Team and seat-based Enterprise plans the spend report covers usage-credit spend only, so this report reads who is buying past their included allowance and how much, not consumption inside it. On usage-based Enterprise plans the report covers all usage.
